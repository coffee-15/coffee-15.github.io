Assoc. Professor Julia A. Fehrer

Director

Julia A. Fehrer is Associate Professor of Innovation and Strategy and serves as Co-Director of the Circular Economy in Business Research Centre at the University of Auckland. Her research forms at the intersection of strategy, service research, and innovation, focusing on market and ecosystem innovation as catalyst for digital and societal transformation.

She is the Academic Lead of the Disciplinay Area Innovation and Strategy, co-chairs a Special Interest Group focused on Market Shaping and Innovation (MASHIN) and contributes to the Journal of Service Management, Journal of Service Theory and Practice, and AMS Review as a member of their editorial boards.
